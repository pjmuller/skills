## Anti-AI-slop checklist (apply to every draft, keep deliberate phrasing the person wrote)
- "Not X but Y" contrasts; one-line dramatic closers that restate the point; rows of fragments.
- Staged openers: "Let's dive in", "Here's the thing", "Honestly?", "Quick note".
- Arguing with nobody: "I'm not saying…", "To be clear", "Some might say".
- Forced triads when one or two examples carry the meaning.
- Stock AI words: crucial, delve, robust, landscape, pivotal, leverage (verb), seamless, elevate,
  journey, empower, unlock, game-changer, testament to, plays a key role, exciting times.
- Bold label on every bullet, decorative headings, curly quotes, em dashes.
- Chatbot residue: "I hope this helps", "Let me know if…", "Would you like…".
- Compliments the sender could not have verified; three "personal" details where one proves it.
Keep: opinions, uneven sentence length, a joke that adds a thought, one specific detail.
